# Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge — Week 2 rules

Source: https://dev.to/challenges/hacktoberfest-week2-2026-10-12 (pulled 2026-10-13)
Background: https://dev.to/mlh/everything-you-need-to-know-about-hacktoberfest-2026-ai-belongs-to-everyone-mdk

## Theme: "Side Hustle"
Build something with open-weight models or open-source AI that someone would actually pay for.
"Think small, specific, and useful. The best builds here solve a real problem for a real person,
whether that's a freelancer, a small business owner, a niche hobby community, or your friend who
keeps complaining about the same tedious task." Examples given: a local invoice and receipt sorter
for freelancers, an Etsy/eBay listing writer from product photos, a menu translator for a local
restaurant.

The write-up says why open innovation matters for this build: cost low enough that the business
works, customer data kept private, fine-tuning for a niche, no lock-in to someone else's pricing.

**Bonus points for landing a first user:** "Did they pay? Would they? What did they ask for next?"

## Hard requirements
- Open-source AI is at the core (open-weight models, open-source agent harnesses or frameworks,
  local inference, or any mix). "The open pieces should be what makes your project work."
- **New project**, started and completed inside the challenge window (opens Oct 12). Pull requests
  to existing projects do not count.
- Commits made after the deadline must be noted in the project README, or the entry can be
  disqualified.
- Non-trivial use of prior work, including open source code, must be credited in the submission.
- Published as a DEV post from the template (`TEMPLATE-week2.md`), tags `devchallenge` and
  `hf26challenge`.
- **Deadline: 2026-10-18, 11:59 PM PDT.** Winners announced the week of Oct 19.
- 18+. Teams of up to 4, one submission, teammates' DEV handles in the post.
- Non-English posts get a completion badge only.
- AI use: "Use of AI is allowed as long as all other rules are followed."
- Optional: save the agent session with DevRelay and embed it with the `agent_session` liquid tag.

## Judging (writing is weighted most)
1. Writing quality ("A great project with a thin write-up won't win.")
2. Relevance to the prompt and theme
3. Creativity
4. Technical execution
5. Use of partner technology (optional)

Ties go to the post with the most positive reactions.

## Prizes (one win per challenge per submission)
- Overall: $250, DEV++ membership, winner badge.
- Featured partners, $200 each: Render, TabPFN, Tinker (fine-tune with a clear improvement in
  performance, latency or cost over a baseline), Arduino (UNO Q), DigitalOcean, **Gemma** ("run it
  locally, fine-tune it, or serve it through Google Cloud or another provider").
- Partners, $100 each: Backboard, ElevenLabs, Entire, GitHub Copilot, Mastra, MongoDB Atlas,
  Sentry Agent Tracing, SerpApi, a .tech domain, Temporal, Tiger Data. 17 categories in all.
- Partner credits: hacktoberfest.com/my/promos (Tinker, Render, Backboard, .tech domain).
